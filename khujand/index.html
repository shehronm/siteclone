The scope

What we
build together.

↗

Structure and copy

Review your offer, organise pages and help explain the services. Agree which materials your team will provide.

⊞

Interface design

Design the visual presentation and visitor journey: finding information, exploring work and getting in touch. Agree the key screens before development.

↔

A responsive website

Check navigation, images, forms and readability at different screen sizes. Visitors should be able to complete their task on phones and computers.

↳

Forms and integrations

Set up the agreed enquiry channel. Connect Telegram, a CRM or a booking service when the project calls for it.

⊕

Search foundations

Configure page titles, descriptions, a sitemap and crawler access. Check the URLs and links between language versions.

✓

Launch and handover

Check links, forms and key journeys, then connect the domain. Agree how content will be updated and what ongoing support covers.

Questions & answers

Before
we begin.

01

Does MIRWINK have an office in Khujand?

+

We work from Khujand online and do not have a client-facing office. We discuss projects through messages and calls and share designs and working versions by link.

02

How much does a website cost?

+

Pricing depends on the pages, design, features and integrations. Send a brief and examples so we can clarify the scope and prepare a proposal. Domain, hosting and paid services are discussed separately.

03

How long does development take?

+

We agree a timeline after reviewing the scope and materials. A service landing page and a store with integrations require different amounts of work. Stages and timing are agreed before development begins.

04

Can you improve an existing website?

+

Yes. We review the site and your goals, then identify changes to the structure, mobile layout, copy, forms or integrations. We agree a specific scope before making changes.

05

Do you work only with clients in Khujand?

+

We are based in Khujand and can work online with clients in other cities. Communication, content languages and the review process are discussed at the start.

06

Can you connect CRM, Telegram or AI tools?

+

Yes, these are MIRWINK service areas. First we define the task: receiving enquiries, managing customers or working with information. Integration options depend on the selected systems and available connections.
