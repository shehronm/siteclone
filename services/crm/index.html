When it makes sense

Close the gaps
in everyday work.

01 /

Enquiries arrive everywhere

Website forms, private conversations and spreadsheets create several versions of the same story. We bring agreed sources into a shared process with clear routing rules.

02 /

Nobody owns the next step

A deal can have a status without a planned action. We define ownership, tasks and reminders so handoffs do not depend on someone remembering to follow up.

03 /

Information is copied by hand

A contact moves from a chat to a spreadsheet and then into a report. We design integrations, required-field checks and duplicate handling before information enters the workflow.

04 /

Reports lack context

An enquiry count alone cannot explain your sales process. We define stages and loss reasons that help the team understand where work is waiting and what needs attention.

Questions & answers

Before
we begin.

01

Do we have to replace our current CRM?

+

Not necessarily. We first review what works, what is missing and which integrations are available. Adjusting stages and connecting existing tools may be enough. A migration should solve a specific problem and justify its cost.

02

Which CRM do you use?

+

Twenty is one possible foundation. The platform, hosting and connections are selected after reviewing requirements. Compatibility, licences and third-party running costs are clarified before implementation.

03

Can you connect our website and Telegram?

+

Yes, this is a core use case. We first check available APIs, permissions and data structures. Then we agree what is transferred, where records are created and how the team learns about integration failures.

04

How are cost and delivery time determined?

+

They depend on workflows, connections, source data quality, migration scope and access requirements. After the discovery conversation, we provide a scope, milestones and estimate. Third-party licences and running costs are listed separately.

05

What should we prepare for the first conversation?

+

Describe where enquiries come from, who works on them and where the process becomes difficult. An anonymised record or spreadsheet can help. Access to production systems is not needed for the first conversation.

06

How do you measure the outcome?

+

Before rollout, we agree metrics and a measurement method. Afterwards, we compare comparable periods: response time, record completeness and enquiries with a next step. Sales also depend on the offer, traffic and team, so we do not promise a universal percentage.
