When it makes sense

Close the gaps
in everyday work.

01 /

Enquiries stay buried in conversations

A message shows interest, but lacks the details needed for the next step. We design a short flow that collects the necessary information, lets the customer review it and passes it to the right person or system.

02 /

Your team repeats the same questions

Which service does the customer need, when are they available and how can you contact them? These questions recur in every conversation. We put straightforward steps into the bot and leave unusual situations for a conversation with a specialist.

03 /

A simple bot becomes difficult to use

A large catalogue, filters and forms can be awkward in a chain of messages. When the task needs visual choices, we design a Mini App inside Telegram and use the bot for access and relevant notifications.

04 /

Conversations are separate from business systems

People copy information manually and struggle to reconstruct the status of a request. We define what should reach the CRM or schedule, how it is updated and what happens if the transfer fails.

05 /

Customers do not know what happens next

After sending a message, the customer cannot tell whether the request was received or who is handling it. We design explicit confirmations and statuses, with a route to a person when the automated flow is not enough.

Practical use cases

Your challenge.
Your team.

01

A service enquiry connected to CRM

The customer selects a service, answers the necessary questions and reviews their details. The service sends the enquiry to the agreed system, routes further handling according to defined rules and tells the customer whether submission succeeded.

↗
02

Booking against an available schedule

The customer selects a service, specialist or time. Where a suitable integration is available, the service checks the scheduling system and rechecks availability before confirmation. Rescheduling, cancellation and manual approval rules are defined in advance.

↗
03

A catalogue or order form in a Mini App

The customer explores the offer, chooses options and submits a request through an interface inside Telegram. Payment integration is discussed separately if the process needs it; an enquiry or order awaiting approval does not require it.

↗
04

Service requests and human handover

The bot helps the customer choose a topic, describe the issue and route the request to the appropriate team. We configure notifications for agreed stages. When a question falls outside the flow, the customer has a clear way to reach a specialist.

Questions & answers

Before
we begin.

01

Should we choose a bot or a Mini App?

+

A bot works well for short conversations, notifications and simple data collection. A Mini App is useful for catalogues, filters, several related fields or visual choices. Sometimes they work together. We choose based on the customer's task, rather than the number of features.

02

Can it connect to the CRM we already use?

+

We first check its API, access rules and the operations you need. We then agree the enquiry structure, field mapping, status updates and duplicate handling. Having a CRM does not by itself establish that every integration is available.

03

Do we have to include payments?

+

No. The service can collect enquiries, book appointments, help customers choose and hand them over to a team member without taking payments in Telegram. If payments are needed, we separately review their place in the process, an available integration and current platform requirements.

04

Can customers reschedule or cancel a booking?

+

That workflow can be included if the scheduling system supports it. Before implementation, we agree restrictions, availability checks and the cases in which a team member must approve the change.

05

What happens if a customer stops midway or the CRM is unavailable?

+

We address these cases during design. For an unfinished flow, we decide which information is saved and how the customer can continue. For integration failures, we define notifications, permitted retries and manual handling. A successful transfer should not be confirmed before it has been checked.
